About me

Hi, I'm Breanna.

Who I am

I grew up in San Diego and spent four years finding my footing at UC Berkeley. Somewhere between research, jobs, club soccer, event planning, Greek life, a double major, and more side quests than I can count, I developed a habit of asking questions about everything.

That's what led me to study Data Science and Cognitive Science. I've always been fascinated by people. How do we think? Why do we behave the way we do? How do our relationships, environments, and experiences shape us? The more I learned, the more I realized that the questions I cared about most weren't just about data or technology. They were about humans.

Looking back, that curiosity has shaped almost everything I've done. Research, teaching, community building, event planning, and countless conversations with people whose lives look nothing like mine. More than anything, I'm someone who loves learning, asking questions, and following ideas wherever they lead.

How I work

I've always been happiest when my calendar is a little too full.

At Berkeley, that looked like balancing three jobs, research, Greek life, a double major, and whatever side quest I had accidentally signed myself up for that week (shoutout to spontaneously summiting Half Dome in the dark). I showed up for 6:45 a.m. opening shifts, spent late nights working on projects, and somehow convinced myself this was a reasonable way to live.

The truth is, I love building things with people. I thrive in fast-moving environments, enjoy solving problems as they come up, and like being the person others can count on when something needs to get done. I care about details, not because I'm a perfectionist (okay, maybe a little), but because details are what turn good experiences into memorable ones.
