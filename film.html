On serendipity
Origin story

How a stranger at a gym
changed the way I see.

I've always loved the idea of preserving the little moments that make life feel meaningful.

What started with disposable cameras turned into a deeper love for film after a serendipitous encounter at UC Berkeley's RSF gym, where a fellow film enthusiast gifted me my first camera. A Samsung Maxima Zoom 90i.

Since then, I've rarely left home without a camera in hand.

Welcome to the journey.

There's a reason I connect film photography to 222's mission. Both are about creating the conditions for something to happen. Then getting out of the way. The best photos I've taken were accidents. A stranger. The right light. Being present enough to notice. 222 is building infrastructure for exactly that. I've been documenting it, one frame at a time, and I'd love to be a part of the team that sees the world in the same light.
